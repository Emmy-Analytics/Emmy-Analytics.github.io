PROJECT 03 · PRODUCT MANAGEMENT

Reducing Lost & Stuck Parcels Through AI Sorting Monitoring

Shopee Express DC Cakung case study exploring how Computer Vision can improve real-time visibility during parcel sorting.

TYPE Academic Study Case
FOCUS Product Management · AI · Logistics
STATUS Completed

01 · CONTEXT

A logistics problem hiding inside the sorting process.

This case study explores how AI-driven automation could improve warehouse sorting visibility and reduce parcels that become lost, stuck, or mis-routed.

02 · FIELD EVIDENCE

What I found from reviews and field interviews.

8 / 8 Google Maps reviews mentioning lost or stuck parcels.
100% Of the selected review examples showed a similar lost/stuck issue.
800–900 Packages reported as being processed daily by worker.
~100 Packages reported as having some kind of issue in a day.

“Banyak barang waktu proses unpacking itu tercecer, lama ngendep, pas nemu udah ga bisa diproses.”

— Cakung DC EH / Tracer worker

03 · ROOT CAUSE

The problem is not only people. It is visibility.

People

Operators cannot continuously monitor every package across a large sorting area.

Process

Sorting and monitoring are still heavily manual, making problems reactive rather than preventive.

Technology

CCTV is mainly used for investigation after an incident, rather than real-time detection.

Measurement

There is no real-time last-seen visibility for parcels during sorting.

ROOT CAUSE Lack of real-time monitoring throughout the sorting process.

04 · PRODUCT OPPORTUNITY

Move from reactive investigation to proactive monitoring.

The proposed product uses Computer Vision with CCTV to detect sorting anomalies in real time and notify the supervisor before a parcel becomes a larger exception.

AS-IS · REACTIVE

Inbound → Manual Sorting → Parcel falls / stuck / mis-routed → Outbound → Return → Customer complaint → Compensation

TO-BE · PROACTIVE

Inbound → AI Monitoring → Real-time Alert → SPV Action → Outbound

05 · AI MONITORING PILOT PROJECT

Start small, validate the system, then scale.

6 CCTV cameras integrated with AI
1 Sorting → outbound focus area
3 Key anomaly types: fallen, stuck, mis-routed

ABOUT THE PILOT

Start with a focused monitoring area.

The pilot focuses on the sorting area toward outbound, where parcels can become fallen, stuck, or mis-routed during the process.

Six CCTV cameras are positioned across the selected area and connected to an AI monitoring layer that continuously observes the sorting flow.

01 Detect

AI identifies abnormal parcel movement or a possible exception.

02 Alert

The system surfaces the issue for supervisor review.

03 Act

The supervisor verifies and responds before the parcel becomes a larger exception.

08 · CONCLUSION

Better visibility can prevent bigger operational problems.

This case study proposes a shift from reactive parcel investigation toward proactive monitoring using Computer Vision.

The objective is not to replace warehouse workers, but to give operators and supervisors earlier visibility so they can respond before a parcel becomes lost, stuck, or mis-routed.

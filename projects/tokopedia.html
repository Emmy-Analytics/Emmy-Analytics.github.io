Exploring where user interest drops across the purchase funnel and identifying opportunities to improve purchase conversion.

01 · BUSINESS PROBLEM

User interest is increasing, but purchase conversion remains low.

The analysis focuses on understanding where users drop off between product discovery, wishlist, cart, and completed purchase.

03 · USER BEHAVIOR

What may be stopping users from completing the purchase?

Waiting for discounts

Users may delay purchase until a better promotion becomes available.

Comparing prices

Users may compare prices and shipping costs across platforms.

Unclear stock availability

Product availability can influence whether users continue toward purchase.

Wishlist visibility

The wishlist may be less visible and less actively used than the cart.

04 · OPPORTUNITY

The opportunity is not only more traffic — it is more urgency.

The analysis suggests that users already show purchase intent. The opportunity is to make products easier to discover, increase urgency, and shorten the path from wishlist or cart to purchase.

05 · RECOMMENDATIONS

Turning conversion friction into product opportunities.

Make Wishlist easier to discover +

PRODUCT OPPORTUNITY

Make Wishlist easier to discover

Wishlist activity is already present, but the feature may be less visible and less accessible than the cart.

WHAT I PROPOSE

Surface Wishlist on the homepage and keep it visually close to the cart so users can access saved items more easily.

Expected outcome: higher wishlist engagement and more opportunities to return users to products they saved.

Add checkout directly from Wishlist +

PURCHASE FRICTION

Add checkout directly from Wishlist

Users who save products to Wishlist may still need to move back through the cart before completing a purchase.

WHAT I PROPOSE

Add a direct purchase action on the Wishlist page so users can move from saved item to checkout with fewer steps.

Expected outcome: shorter purchase flow and reduced friction between intent and checkout.

Create stock-based urgency +

PURCHASE URGENCY

Create stock-based urgency

Users may delay purchases when there is no visible signal that product availability is changing.

WHAT I PROPOSE

Show signals such as “Almost out of stock”, “Only 1 left”, and “Back in stock” directly on relevant product experiences.

Expected outcome: stronger urgency and faster movement from browsing to purchase.

Introduce limited-time incentives +

CONVERSION INCENTIVE

Introduce limited-time incentives

Users may already have products in their cart or Wishlist, but still delay the purchase while waiting for discounts or better timing.

WHAT I PROPOSE

Create urgency through two touchpoints: a push notification that brings users back to the product, followed by an in-app voucher with a visible countdown.

Expected outcome: stronger urgency, higher return engagement, and faster purchase conversion.

07 · CONCLUSION

Conversion is not only about more users.

The case study suggests that users already show interest in products, but purchase completion remains a key challenge.

Improving wishlist visibility, reducing purchase friction, and creating timely urgency may help move users from interest toward completed purchases.
